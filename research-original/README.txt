RESEARCH PRACTICE

1. Extract the entire ZIP folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
3. Choose an answer to see immediate feedback and the source slide/page.
4. Select Next question to continue. At the end, expand your mistakes to review them.
5. Restart quiz resets the score and shuffles questions and choices again.

No installation, server, internet connection or account is needed.
Keep index.html, style.css, questions.js and app.js together.
Refreshing or closing the page starts a new session; progress is not saved.
